How to play

Every shot, you choose where to hit, then where to cover your opponent's reply. Your opponent covered a spot with their last shot, so guess where they will not be.

If they guessed right, they read it and get an easier ball. If they guessed wrong, they are pushed around, and a big enough shot is a winner. Risky shots miss more often, especially when you are stretched or tired.

Serving: the returner guesses wide, body, or down the T first. Flat serves are big but miss often. Spin serves are safe. Body serves rarely ace but jam the returner.

Shots: crosscourt is safe. Down the line pushes harder but misses more. A drop shot beats a player stuck on the baseline. A lob beats a player at the net. Approach to come to the net, where volleys hit harder but lobs and passing shots can beat you.
